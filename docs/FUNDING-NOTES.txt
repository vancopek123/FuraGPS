FURA GPS — OPTIONAL SUPPORT

Core navigation is free. No payment account, donation link, subscription,
advertising SDK or in-app purchase is configured in this release.

Possible future support models include voluntary one-time/monthly support,
original dashboard themes, or custom layouts for virtual trucking companies.
These are ideas, not shipped paid features or promises of revenue.

Map-data rights are separate from the MIT application code. A funding link or
public download does not grant permission to rehost or sell third-party maps.
See THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt before distributing commercial bundles.
