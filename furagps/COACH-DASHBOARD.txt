FURA GPS 0.11.1 — DASHBOARD AND ETS2 COACH TOOLS

The dashboard adds game time, RPM, fuel range, cruise control and available
vehicle warnings. Expand More instruments for the extra values. Collapse the
card or disable it in settings when you want more map space.

G-METER VISIBILITY
Automatic: shown during an active ETS2 Coach job; hidden for ordinary trucks.
Always show: manual display, including free driving by bus without an active job.
Hide: hide only the G-meter while keeping other instruments.
Automatic detection uses active Coach job data, not the physical vehicle model.
Coach timetable reading is ETS2 only; it is not enabled in ATS.

WHAT G-FORCE MEANS HERE
The SDK supplies local VEHICLE acceleration. The main number is horizontal
acceleration (lateral plus acceleration/braking) divided by 9.80665 m/s².
The dot shows direction; vertical acceleration and peak values are in the extra
instruments. The plugin retains brief spikes between its approximately 10-Hz
packets. It does not measure individual passengers on their seats.

COMFORT COLORS
Green: smooth. Orange: ease off. Red: too harsh.
Higher levels appear immediately and are held briefly so short spikes stay
visible. Sensitivity is adjustable from 0.5 to 2.5 times. At 1x, approximate
warning thresholds are 0.10 g and 0.20 g. These are adjustable HUD estimates;
the game's exact warning colors, filters and measurement point are not read.
They are not claimed to match the game meter one-to-one.

ADVANCED MODES
Coach penalty limits uses per-axis local/reference penalty definitions, not the
earlier comfort warnings. Disabled axes and unavailable vertical data are handled
explicitly. Multiplayer changes to those definitions are not confirmed.
Custom G threshold compares horizontal G-force to your chosen limit.
Reset peak G clears the peak. Pause/missing telemetry clears live readings.
Demo and live measurements are separate.

LIVE COACH TIMETABLE
The adapter follows the active Coach stage, its next-checkpoint ETA, departure
bounds and rolling timetable correction. It resolves derived arrival/departure
times instead of treating every unset field as a missing schedule. Live values
take priority over an old manual delivery-clock entry.
The display tolerance is inclusive -4 to +4 in-game minutes. Stages mode also
shows early arrival; Game HUD mode counts early arrival as on time.
If live reading is unavailable, SDK/GPS or manual fallback is labelled ESTIMATE.
Manual entry uses the game clock; choose Tomorrow in game across midnight.
Changing game clears the previous game's live Coach scope and manual timetable.

BUILD LIMITS
The exact Coach adapter was checked for ETS2 1.62.3.3, including a previous live
HaulMP stage. Unknown executable/loaded-code changes and inconsistent stage
reads are rejected. That check does not validate all G colors or every server.
See TESTING-0.11.1.txt and GAME-GPS-READER.txt for test coverage and diagnostics.
